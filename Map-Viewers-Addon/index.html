Adds support for Dynmap, BlueMap, Squaremap and Plx3Map.

Map markers and icons. Every kingdom can set their own flag and marker color from nexus settings.

Note

This page is still under review and information is missing.

Warning

LiveAtlas for Dynmap is known to break the zooming limitation function for kingdom markers, which causes the kingdom markers to appear at extreme zoom values. You may either adjust the zoom values manually or just disable the zoom limit by setting it to -1
This can be changed in maps/map.yml, maps/kingdoms.yml and maps/nations.yml configs (Look for the zoom option)
